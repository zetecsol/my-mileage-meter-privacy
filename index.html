Privacy Policy – My Mileage Meter

Developed by: Ravi Thakkar

Brand: Zero Error Technologies & Solutions (ZETS)

Effective Date: 9 November 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how My Mileage Meter handles information when you use the application. We respect your privacy and aim to keep your vehicle and fuel records under your control.

2. Information Stored by the App

The app allows you to enter and store information such as:

  • Vehicle names and registration numbers.
  • Vehicle categories and fuel types.
  • Odometer readings and travel distances.
  • Fuel or charging quantities and expenses.
  • Mileage calculations and fuel history.
  • Optional PUC and insurance expiry dates.

This information is stored locally on your device for the app's functionality.

3. Data Collection and Sharing

My Mileage Meter does not require user registration or login. The app does not use advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services. Vehicle and fuel records are not intentionally transmitted to ZETS or an external server.

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

4. Device Permissions

The current version does not request Camera, Photos/Gallery, Location, Contacts, Phone, or SMS permissions.

5. Backup and Restore

The app provides a manual backup and restore facility using device storage. Backup files are not uploaded to Google Drive or ZETS servers by the app.

You are responsible for keeping backup files secure. If you uninstall the app, separately saved backup files may remain on your device until you delete them.

6. Automatic Device Backup

Android automatic app-data backup is disabled in the current app configuration. Manual backups are separate and remain under your control.

7. Data Deletion

You can delete records using the app's available features. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes its locally stored app data. Separately saved backup files must be deleted manually.

8. Children's Privacy

My Mileage Meter is a general-purpose vehicle management application and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app features or privacy practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
